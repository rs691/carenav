BlueCross Premier PPO — 2026 Claims, Prior Authorization, and Appeals Policy
Effective Date: September 1, 2026

SECTION 1 PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Section 1.1 When Prior Authorization Is Required
Prior authorization (PA) is required for: non-emergency outpatient MRI and CT; elective inpatient admissions; specialty medications on the formulary marked PA Required; certain durable medical equipment over $500; and selected outpatient surgeries.

Section 1.2 How to Check Prior Authorization Status
Members can ask CareNav or call Member Services with the PA reference number.
Statuses include: Pending Review, Approved, Denied, More Information Needed, and Expired.
Approved authorizations are typically valid for 90 days from the approval date unless otherwise stated.
If status is Pending Review, average turnaround is 3 business days for standard requests and 24 hours for urgent requests.

Section 1.3 Specialty Drug Prior Authorization
Specialty drugs such as Humira, Enbrel, Ozempic, and Mounjaro require PA before the first fill.
Clinical criteria generally include diagnosis documentation, step therapy where applicable, and prescribing specialist attestation.

SECTION 2 CLAIM STATUS
Section 2.1 Claim Lifecycle
Claim statuses include: Received, In Process, Paid, Denied, and Appealed.
Most clean in-network claims are processed within 30 days of receipt.
Members can request status with the claim number, date of service, and provider name.

Section 2.2 Common Denial Reasons
Common denial reasons include: service not covered under the plan; missing prior authorization; out-of-network provider with no exception; duplicate claim; billing after the filing deadline; and incomplete coding.
If a claim was denied for missing prior authorization, resubmit after obtaining a retrospective review decision when the service was urgent.

Section 2.3 Appeals
You may appeal a denied claim or denied prior authorization within 180 days of the notice date.
First-level appeals are decided within 30 days for pre-service and 60 days for post-service.
Expedited appeals are available when delay could seriously jeopardize health.

SECTION 3 CONTACT
Section 3.1 Member Services
Member Services is available Monday–Friday 8am–8pm local time.
For complex claim or PA issues, CareNav can escalate to a live benefits specialist.
