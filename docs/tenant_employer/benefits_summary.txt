Acme Corp Benefits — 2026 Summary Plan Description (Excerpt)
Effective Date: September 1, 2026
Plan Name: Acme Corp Benefits

SECTION 1 DEDUCTIBLE
Section 1.1 Plan Deductible
Individual in-network deductible: $1,000.
Family in-network deductible: $2,000.
Out-of-network deductible: $2,000 individual / $4,000 family.

SECTION 2 OUTPATIENT SERVICES
Section 2.1 Diagnostic Imaging Including MRI
Outpatient MRI is covered when medically necessary.
In-network: after deductible, plan pays 90% (you pay 10% coinsurance).
Prior authorization is required for non-emergency MRI.

Section 2.2 Office Visits
Primary care in-network copay: $25.
Specialist in-network copay: $50.

SECTION 3 HOSPITAL
Section 3.1 Inpatient
In-network inpatient: after deductible, plan pays 90%.
Elective admissions require prior authorization.

SECTION 4 PRESCRIPTION DRUGS
Section 4.1 Pharmacy
Acme uses a three-tier formulary administered by the pharmacy benefit manager.
Mail order is available for 90-day maintenance fills.

SECTION 5 CLAIMS
Section 5.1 Filing and Status
In-network providers bill the plan. Claim statuses include Received, Processed, Paid, and Denied.
Appeals must be filed within 180 days of a denial.
