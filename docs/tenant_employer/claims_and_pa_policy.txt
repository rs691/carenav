Acme Corp Benefits — Claims and Prior Authorization Policy
Effective Date: September 1, 2026

SECTION 1 PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Section 1.1 Scope
Prior authorization is required for non-emergency MRI, elective inpatient care, and medications flagged PA Required on the formulary.

Section 1.2 Status
Members may check PA status with the authorization number. Typical statuses: Pending Review, Approved, Denied.

SECTION 2 CLAIMS
Section 2.1 Denials and Appeals
Common denial reasons include missing PA, out-of-network without exception, and timely filing limits.
Appeals are accepted for 180 days after the denial notice.
