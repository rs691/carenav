Illinois Medicaid — 2026 Covered Benefits Overview
Effective Date: September 1, 2026
Plan Name: Illinois Medicaid

SECTION 1 COST SHARING
Section 1.1 Copays and Deductible
Most covered services have $0 or nominal copays under Illinois Medicaid.
There is no annual deductible for eligible members.

SECTION 2 OUTPATIENT SERVICES
Section 2.1 Diagnostic Imaging Including MRI
Medically necessary outpatient MRI is covered when ordered by an enrolled provider.
Prior authorization is required for non-emergency MRI and CT.
Emergency imaging performed in an emergency department is covered without prior authorization.

Section 2.2 Primary Care and Specialists
Primary care visits with enrolled providers are covered.
Specialist visits require a referral from your primary care provider except for emergency care and certain women's health services.

SECTION 3 HOSPITAL
Section 3.1 Inpatient Care
Inpatient hospital stays are covered when medically necessary.
Elective admissions require prior authorization.

SECTION 4 PRESCRIPTION DRUGS
Section 4.1 Formulary
Covered drugs are listed on the Illinois Medicaid formulary.
Preferred generics are encouraged. Some brand and specialty drugs require prior authorization.

SECTION 5 PREVENTIVE CARE
Section 5.1 Preventive Services
Age-appropriate preventive screenings and immunizations are covered at no cost to the member.
