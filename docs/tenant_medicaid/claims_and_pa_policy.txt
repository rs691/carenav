Illinois Medicaid — Prior Authorization Guidance
Effective Date: September 1, 2026

SECTION 1 PRIOR AUTHORIZATION
Section 1.1 Required Services
Prior authorization is required for non-emergency MRI/CT, elective inpatient admissions, and non-preferred formulary drugs marked PA Required.

Section 1.2 Status Check
PA statuses: Pending, Approved, Denied, Additional Information Needed.
Standard decisions are typically issued within 3 business days.

SECTION 2 CLAIMS
Section 2.1 Claim Status
Enrolled providers bill Medicaid directly. Members rarely file paper claims.
If a service is denied, ask the provider for the denial reason code and appeal rights.
